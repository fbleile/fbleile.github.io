Stationary Diffusions

An interactive starting point for the notebook. Watch a cloud of particles evolve under simple two-dimensional dynamics. The explanatory text will be developed later.

All particles follow a linear drift toward the origin. t = 0.0

Three dynamics

Reverting ODE: dXt = −θXt dt   ·   Brownian motion: dXt = σdWt   ·   Stationary diffusion: dXt = −θXtdt + σdWt

The first process contracts the cloud toward one point. Brownian motion spreads without a stationary distribution. Combining the two creates a Gaussian stationary distribution: noise keeps the particles moving while the drift keeps them near the origin.
